# Generation briefs

Tool: built-in image_gen. Both assets request transparent_background=true; no door/lantern/background drawn into the VFX.

Gold glints: one native128×64 sparse sprite, seven separable warm gold glints clustered around(64,38), inside x44..84/y27..48. Three3×3 four-point glints, two tiny2×2 motes, two single pixels. Muted gold/amber and pale gold centers, hard square pixels, no white bloom/neon/magic rings/text. Intended as a gentle opened-mine-exit indicator, not an attack. Native pixels retained if enlarged. Source glints were subsequently separated and normalized for authored motion.

Tunnel glow: one independent128×64 transparent pixel-art patch limited to native x42..87/y25..52; subtle shallow trapezoid amber/gold inside the mine tunnel, brightest dim bottom-center(64,48), lower alpha toward top/sides. Few flat stepped pixel bands, no airbrush blur/AA/neon/beam/ring/particles/door/wood/stone. Intended not to obscure dark tunnel supports. Final native range/alpha are specified in the validated game PNG.
